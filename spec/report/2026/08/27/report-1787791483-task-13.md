### Tooling/process

- Regenerating facade files via string-transform of a sibling provider is
  error-prone: a `(?:async )?def` regex matched sync methods during the
  async pass and silently overwrote the sync tier (caught by mypy/ruff,
  then rewritten with distinct sync/async matchers). Lesson for the
  cross-provider tooling story: prefer explicit per-method generation or
  golden-file diffing over blind transforms.
- CapMonster's single task-type names (no proxyless/proxy split) mean the
  adapter is simpler than the other two — no type-conditional logic except
  v3's enterprise split.
